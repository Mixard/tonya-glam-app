Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy describes how Tonya glam analytics ("the app") handles data. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Tonya's glam.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly to read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel: views, watch time, traffic sources, YouTube search terms and thumbnail impressions. Access is read-only. The app cannot upload, edit or delete anything on the channel.

How the data is used

The data is used only to analyse the channel's performance and to help the owner write video titles and descriptions. It is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

Storage and sharing

The access token and downloaded reports are stored on a private server controlled by the channel team. The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the app can no longer read any data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the Tonya's glam channel page.
